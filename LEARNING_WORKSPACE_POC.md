# Learning workspace POC (do not merge)

Experiments on top of the new reader (`reader-next/`) to make learning more convenient.

- Per-book bookmarks that track your position: bookmarks page, manual "move bookmark here", automatic tracking.
- Resizable panels and stacked blocks (e.g. Talmud on the right, Rashi above Tosafot on the left).
- Collapsible header and full-screen mode.
- Right-click menu on text: move bookmark, word translation, favourite commentaries, and more.

Cauldron: https://learning-workspace-poc.cauldron.sefaria.org
