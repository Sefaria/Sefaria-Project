# Bookmark progress POC (do not merge)

One bookmark per book that records where the reader is in that book.

- Bookmarks page: every bookmarked book with its current position and a resume link.
- Manual mode: right-click in the reader, "Move bookmark here".
- Automatic mode: the bookmark follows reading progress.

Cauldron: https://bookmark-progress-poc.cauldron.sefaria.org
