## Legacy approach vs new approach

**The assistant and the reader.**

- **Legacy (`master` @ `e18dacc0d`)**: the assistant is the `<lc-chatbot>` web component from `Sefaria/ai-chatbot` (Svelte, `customElement`), loaded by a
  script tag (`templates/base.html:75-76`, URL from `sefaria/system/context_processors.py:125-172`) and mounted by `ReaderApp` on desktop
  Library pages with a user token (`static/js/ReaderApp.jsx:2586-2588`, `:2621-2633`). It steers the reader by dispatching
  `sefaria:bootstrap-url` (`Sefaria/ai-chatbot` @ `b2d3c8a` `src/components/LCChatbot.svelte:1487`, `:1694`, `:1708`) and reads where the reader is from
  `window.location.href` (`:1064`, `src/lib/api.js:70`). Its own `chatbot:*` events have no listener in the reader.
- **New (`reader-next`)**: not mounted, and no listener for its events (AI-011 not started). Full inventory:
  `../routing/architecture.md` → Integrations.
