The Seven Days

Genesis 1:1–2:3 · scroll the story or the text

Day one
יְהִי אוֹר

Light

And God said: ‘Let there be light.’ And there was light.
Genesis 1:3
Day two
יְהִי רָקִיעַ

The firmament

Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.
Genesis 1:6
Day three
וְתֵרָאֶה הַיַּבָּשָׁה

Land and plants

Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear.
Genesis 1:9
Day four
יְהִי מְאֹרֹת

The lights

Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons.
Genesis 1:14
Day five
יִשְׁרְצוּ הַמַּיִם

Fish and birds

Let the waters swarm with swarms of living creatures, and let fowl fly above the earth.
Genesis 1:20
Day six
בְּצַלְמוֹ

Animals and humankind

And God created man in His own image, in the image of God created He him; male and female created He them.
Genesis 1:27
The seventh day
וַיִּשְׁבֹּת

Rest

And on the seventh day God finished His work which He had made; and He rested on the seventh day.
Genesis 2:2
